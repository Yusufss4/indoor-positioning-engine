Live on your phone

The same engine, fed by this phone's motion sensors. Hold the phone in front of you, screen up, its top edge pointing where you walk. A web page cannot read Wi-Fi or Bluetooth signal strength, so the position comes from your steps, the compass and the walls of the map, starting from the point you tap. Back to the demo

The green dot is the engine's estimate, the green squares are where it holds probability. A tap on the map after the start leaves a black square: "I am really here". The error at each one is shown, and the saved recording keeps them, so the walk can be replayed and scored with ipe-replay. Engine load is the time the engine needs per second of sensor data.
