Indoor Positioning Engine

The C++ engine compiled to WebAssembly: a simulated walker, BLE beacons, phone IMU and barometer, fused by a particle filter (red), an EKF (blue) and a weighted-centroid baseline (green). Everything runs in your browser. It opens in 3D on a simulated walk through a real seven-floor university building imported from OpenStreetMap; the "Real recordings" scenarios replay phone walks from two shopping malls over their floor plans. Source on GitHub

Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, double-click to fit.
