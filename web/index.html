Indoor Positioning Engine

The C++ engine compiled to WebAssembly: a simulated walker, BLE beacons, phone IMU and barometer, fused by a grid Bayes filter (green dot). It holds a probability for every 0.5 m cell of the floor plan, drawn as green squares, darker (and in 3D taller) with more probability. Everything runs in your browser. It opens in 3D on a simulated walk through a real seven-floor university building imported from OpenStreetMap. The "Real recordings" replay phone data over the buildings' floor plans: walks in two shopping malls, and multi-floor walks with stairs and a barometer in a museum and a university building (IPIN competition). Rooms

    Enter/exit events, newest first. The filter enters a room once its probability (the mass in the room's cells) stays above 60% for 1.5 s, and exits once it stays below 30% for 1.5 s. Truth events come from the true position without delay. In 2D the filter's room is tinted green and the true room is dashed.
